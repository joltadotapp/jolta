Email and calendar, up to speed.

Meet Jolta. An email and calendar app that keeps your conversations and your day moving, with direct connections to the accounts you already use.

Download for MacFree · macOS 26 or later · Apple silicon

Your mail, together.

Connect mailboxes from Microsoft 365, iCloud, Gmail, Google Workspace and IMAP to read, send and organise your email.

Make room for your day.

Connect your calendars to view your schedule and manage events alongside your email.

A direct connection.

Jolta connects from your device to your providers. Your email and calendar data do not pass through servers operated by Layer7 BV.

Open by design.

Jolta is MIT licensed. Jolta’s source code is publicly available on GitHub for anyone to inspect.
