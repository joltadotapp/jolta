Fast mail, with nobody in between.

Jolta is a keyboard-first mail app for Mac. It talks straight to Gmail, Microsoft 365, iCloud and IMAP, so no Jolta server ever stores, relays or reads your mail.

Download for Mac Free · macOS 15.4 or later · Apple silicon. Windows and Linux are planned.

Try it here: press E until all eight are archived, and see the sky Jolta shows at inbox zero. The mail is invented.

Move without the mouse

Every list in Jolta moves with J and K. The focused message shows its own shortcuts.

E

Archive, then undo

E archives on your provider's server. Z brings the message back.

Snooze until it matters

Type "tomorrow 9am". Snoozed mail waits on the server and comes back on time.

Tab

Sections for VIPs, your team and newsletters. Tab steps through them.

The only stop is your Mac.

Jolta syncs each account straight from Google, Microsoft, iCloud or your IMAP server. Your mail and its search index stay on your Mac, and sign-ins stay in the macOS Keychain. AI is off until you pick a model that runs on your Mac, or your own OpenAI or Anthropic key.

  • 0.3 seconds from launch to the first rows of your inbox.
  • 1 millisecond to search a real mailbox of 84,000 messages.
  • 0 of 25 remote images and trackers loaded from a hostile test message.
  • Measured on a Mac mini with a release build.
