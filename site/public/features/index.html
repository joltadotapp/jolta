Triage

    Also in Jolta.

    • Unified and split inbox

      One inbox across every account, with sections for VIPs, your team and newsletters. Tab steps through them.

    • Undo send and send later

      Every message waits ten seconds before it goes, and scheduled mail goes out at the time you pick.

    • Follow-up reminders and snippets

      Get a nudge when nobody has answered, and insert text you type often from ⌘K.

    • Notifications that matter

      By default, Jolta notifies you only about VIPs, replies in your conversations, invitations and mail sent straight to you.

    • Calendar

      Google Calendar, Microsoft 365, CalDAV and webcal feeds in the same app, off per account until you turn it on.

    • AI on your terms

      Conversation summaries and Write with AI (⌘J), with a model that runs on your Mac or your own OpenAI or Anthropic key. Off until you choose.

    • Your AI agents

      Claude Code and other agents can search, archive and draft through Jolta's MCP server. Sending always waits for your approval.

    • Every account

      Gmail and Google Workspace, Microsoft 365 work and school, iCloud, Fastmail and any IMAP server.
